Joint work with [Mee Seong Im](https://sites.google.com/site/meeseongim/) and [Mikhail Khovanov](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~khovanov/).

A commutative Frobenius algebra is the same thing as a two-dimensional topological quantum field theory. In this paper we construct examples of rank-two Frobenius algebras over Dedekind domains, such as rings of integers of number fields, that are projective but not free as modules, and we discuss how such algebras might be used in link homology.

Published in *Inverse Problems, Geometric and Topological Aspects of Representation Theory, and Low-Dimensional Topology*, Contemporary Mathematics, vol. 834, American Mathematical Society, 2026.
