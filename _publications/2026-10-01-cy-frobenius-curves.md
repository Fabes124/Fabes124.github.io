A commutative Frobenius algebra in quasi-coherent sheaves on a scheme $X$ is a two-dimensional topological quantum field theory valued in $\mathrm{QCoh}(X)$. This project asks when such an algebra can be made compatible with Serre duality, and classifies the resulting structures on algebraic curves and on spectra of rings of integers. The answer depends strongly on the genus: in genus at least two the structures are rigid, while in genus zero and one they are governed by torsion data on the curve. Over rings of integers, the analogous structures are controlled by the class group.
