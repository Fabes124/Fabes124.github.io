A commutative Frobenius algebra sheaf on a curve $X$ with trace valued in $\omega_X$ is the same as a finite flat cover $Y \to X$ with $\omega_Y \cong \mathcal{O}_Y$ together with a trivialization. I call these K-trivial covers.

If $X$ has genus at least two, K-trivial covers are never reduced, even over stable curves, and never smoothable. Covers with curvilinear fibers are the split ropes on $(n-1)$-spin bundles, and their moduli space is the Hodge bundle over the moduli of spin curves, also over Chiodo's compactification. In every degree a K-trivial cover degenerates to the split double $\mathcal{O}_Z \oplus \omega_Z$ of a cover $Z$ of at most half the degree, with a core of rank at most two on each component. Over $X$ the core is zero, a theta characteristic or a Prym datum. The paper also classifies the covers of degree five supported on $X$.
