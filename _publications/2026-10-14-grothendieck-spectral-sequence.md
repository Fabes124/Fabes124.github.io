Given left exact functors $F$ and $G$ between abelian categories, the Grothendieck spectral sequence computes the right derived functors of $G \circ F$ from those of $F$ and $G$. This note reviews derived functors and the spectral sequence of a filtered complex, builds the Grothendieck spectral sequence from a Cartan–Eilenberg resolution, and derives the five-term exact sequence. It then specializes to the Leray, Lyndon–Hochschild–Serre, and change-of-rings spectral sequences, with explicit computations including examples with nonzero differentials.
