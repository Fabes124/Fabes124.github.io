Joint work with Sabrina Jia.

A commutative Frobenius algebra over $\mathbb{Z}$ that is an algebra over a ring of integers $\mathcal{O}_K$ is the same as a finite flat cover of $\operatorname{Spec} \mathcal{O}_K$ with trivial dualizing sheaf over $\mathbb{Z}$. The inverse different plays the role that the canonical bundle plays on a curve.

In rank two, the classes realized by such covers form the coset $[\mathfrak{D}]^{-1}\,\mathrm{Cl}(K)[2]$, by Hecke's theorem. For imaginary quadratic fields $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})$ with $d \equiv 3 \pmod 4$, $d \neq -1$, unramified covers miss half of these classes, and the cyclotomic $\mathbb{Z}_2$-tower supplies missing ones: each step is a cover realizing the class of the prime above 2, never unramified, which capitulates at the next step. The Selmer group that computes the unramified classes also governs K-trivial covers of curves over number fields.
