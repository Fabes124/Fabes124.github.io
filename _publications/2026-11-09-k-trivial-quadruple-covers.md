Degree four is the first degree in which K-trivial covers need not have curvilinear fibers, and the covers of a fixed curve form families of unbounded dimension. This sequel classifies the K-trivial quadruple covers of a curve of genus at least two. There are two types: relative complete intersections of two quadrics, and $\omega_X$-twisted orthogonal bundles, which are parametrized by translates of Prym varieties.

The paper then determines the irreducible components of the moduli stack of trivialized K-trivial quadruple covers, its singular locus and its local structure. The stack is reduced in genus two and over Petri-general curves.
