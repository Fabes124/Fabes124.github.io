Over a ring of integers, the Frobenius algebra sheaves of the companion project are governed by an arithmetic torsion coset in the class group. This sequel follows that coset up a $\mathbb{Z}_2$-extension of the base field and asks when it is stable, in particular whether the associated ideal classes capitulate in the tower.
