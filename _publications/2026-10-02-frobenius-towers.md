This sequel studies the Frobenius algebras over rings of integers that appear in the project above, which are tied to torsion in the class group, and asks how they behave as one moves up a $\mathbb{Z}_2$-extension of the base field. In particular, it addresses whether the associated ideal classes capitulate in the tower.
