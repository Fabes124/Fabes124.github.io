A commutative Frobenius algebra in quasi-coherent sheaves on a scheme $X$ is a two-dimensional topological quantum field theory valued in $\mathrm{QCoh}(X)$. This project classifies cyclic Frobenius algebra sheaves whose trace is compatible with Serre duality on a Dedekind scheme $X$, such as an algebraic curve or the spectrum of a ring of integers. The possible structures are governed by a coset of torsion in the Picard group. On curves of genus at least two they are rigid and tied to spin structures, and over rings of integers they are controlled by the class group.
